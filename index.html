Recipe & Ingredient Specimen

A transparent look inside our slow-simmered Traditional Palm Butter Soup.

Specialty No. 01

Traditional Palm Butter Soup

West African Heritage • Slow-Simmered Fresh Palm Nut Cream

Fresh oil palm nuts are boiled and pounded in a wooden mortar to extract the rich, creamy red butter. Simmered for hours with smoked catfish and tender beef until a glossy, thick stew emerges.

Key Harvest Ingredients

  • Fresh African Oil Palm Nuts (Pulp)
  • Smoked Dry Catfish & Blue Crab
  • Braised Beef & Goat Meat
  • Habanero Peppers & Country Onions

Culinary Profile

  • Simmer Time: 3–4 Hours
  • Serving Style: Poured Over Hot Country Rice
  • Dietary: Dairy-Free • Gluten-Free
  • Heat Level: Bold & Spicy
Calories 520 kcal
Protein 28g
Healthy Fats 32g
